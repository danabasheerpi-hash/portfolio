Project Overview

The Blinkit Sales Dashboard provides a comprehensive overview of retail sales performance across different outlet types, locations, product categories, and customer ratings. The dashboard helps businesses monitor KPIs and identify opportunities to improve sales performance.

Business Problem

Retail businesses generate large amounts of sales data every day. Analysing outlet performance, product categories, customer preferences, and inventory manually is time-consuming. This dashboard centralises key retail metrics into one interactive report to support business decision-making.

Project Objectives

  • Analyse total sales.
  • Monitor average sales.
  • Track outlet performance.
  • Compare item categories.
  • Analyse outlet size and location.
  • Monitor customer ratings.
  • Support retail business decisions.

Business Insights

  • Tier 3 outlets generated the highest overall sales, indicating strong customer demand in these locations.
  • Supermarket Type 1 contributed the highest revenue among all outlet types.
  • Fruits & Vegetables and Snack Foods were among the best-performing product categories.
  • Customer ratings remained consistently high, reflecting positive customer satisfaction.
  • Outlet size and location significantly influenced sales performance and profitability.
  • The dashboard helps managers monitor KPIs, optimise inventory, and identify business growth opportunities.
