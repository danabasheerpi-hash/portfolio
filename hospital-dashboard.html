Project Overview

The Hospital Emergency Room Dashboard provides hospital administrators with a comprehensive view of emergency department operations. It enables healthcare professionals to monitor patient flow, improve operational efficiency, reduce waiting times, and support better patient care through interactive visualisations and KPI tracking.

Business Problem

Hospitals receive a large number of emergency patients every day. Without a centralized reporting system, analysing patient waiting times, admission status, referrals, and operational performance becomes difficult. This dashboard was developed to provide real-time insights into emergency room performance and support informed decision-making.

Project Objectives

  • Monitor emergency room patient volume.
  • Track average waiting time.
  • Analyse patient satisfaction.
  • Monitor admission status.
  • Analyse referrals by department.
  • Study patient demographics.
  • Improve operational efficiency through KPI reporting.

Key Performance Indicators

Monitor the total number of emergency room visits.

Average Wait Time

Track patient waiting time before receiving treatment.

Patient Satisfaction

Measure the overall patient satisfaction score.

Admission Status

Analyse admitted versus non-admitted patients.

Department Referrals

Identify referrals across different hospital departments.

Patients Seen Within 30 Minutes

Evaluate emergency room response efficiency.

Patient Demographics

Analyse age group, gender and race distribution.

Peak Days & Hours

Identify the busiest periods in the emergency department.

Business Insights

  • Peak patient arrivals occurred during specific hours, helping identify staffing requirements.
  • Most patients were treated within the target waiting time, indicating efficient emergency room operations.
  • Department referral analysis highlighted the departments receiving the highest number of emergency referrals.
  • Patient demographics revealed differences in age groups and visit patterns.
  • Patient satisfaction scores helped evaluate the quality of healthcare services.
  • The dashboard supports data-driven decisions to improve hospital efficiency and patient care.
