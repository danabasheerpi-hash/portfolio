Project Overview

The Retail Sales Analytics Dashboard provides business leaders with a comprehensive view of sales performance, product trends, customer insights, and profitability. It enables data-driven decisions through interactive reports and KPI tracking.

Business Problem

Retail businesses generate large volumes of transactional data. Without a centralized reporting solution, analysing sales, profit, customers, and products becomes inefficient. This dashboard consolidates key business metrics into one interactive view to support faster and more informed decision-making.

Project Objectives

  • Monitor overall sales performance.
  • Analyse profit and profit margin.
  • Track customer behaviour.
  • Identify top-performing products.
  • Compare sales by region.
  • Monitor monthly sales trends.
  • Support business growth using KPI reporting.

Tools & Technologies

📊 Power BI

Power Query, DAX, Data Cleaning, Data Modelling, Dashboard Development

Business Insights

  • Regional analysis highlighted the highest-performing sales locations.
  • Top-performing products contributed significantly to overall revenue.
  • Customer segmentation identified valuable repeat customers.
  • Monthly sales trends revealed seasonal demand patterns.
  • Profit analysis helped identify high-margin product categories.
  • The dashboard enables business leaders to monitor KPIs and make data-driven strategic decisions.
